# PowerBox problem statement

When the power goes out, households need a small, safe store of electricity for lights, phones, radio and internet, charged from whatever source is available: human power, a solar panel or the grid when it is up. Design with, not for: requirements must come from co-design sessions and field trials with the intended users through a local partner.

## Users and context

_To be developed._

## Constraints

- Garage-buildable prototype, about $450 USD

## User research and co-design

This design is for communities the author is not part of, so requirements come from the people who will use it.

- [ ] Identify a local partner organization (Helpful Engineering network, NGO or university)
- [ ] Run co-design sessions with intended users; record who, where and what was learned
- [ ] Validate load, distance, terrain and cost assumptions in the field
- [ ] Revise requirements (REQ) from findings before freezing the design
