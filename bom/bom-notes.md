# BOM notes

Prices are indicative concept estimates (TRL 2) and will be confirmed with named suppliers at TRL 3. Item numbers match the exploded view (`media/exploded.png`) and the components table in `docs/02-concept.md`. Items 16 to 18 are in the BOM but not modelled.

**The SwapCell pack is not included in the total.** Line 4 is listed at $0.00 because the pack is designed, costed and budgeted in the SwapCell project (about $370 in prototype parts), and the same pack is shared with SunSpoke and the SwapCell dock. PowerBox parts total about **$435**, within the $450 concept budget with a thin margin of about $15. A household buying a PowerBox with its own pack would spend about **$805** in parts, which is above the $450 budget. Whether the budget should include a pack is proposed, awaiting Amish (see `docs/REVIEW.md`).

The grid charger (line 17, $50) is the same class of certified 54.6 V, 5 A charger as the SwapCell dock charger. A household or charging point that already has a SwapCell dock can omit it, bringing the PowerBox parts to about $385.

The largest cost drivers are the inverter ($70), the output panel ($45) and protection and wiring ($35). A DC-only variant without the inverter and AC outlet would save about $92 and remove all mains voltage from the box.
